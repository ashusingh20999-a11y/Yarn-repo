What we inspect

Every parameter tells a story.

Inspection scope is tailored to yarn type, buyer requirements, sampling plan and the agreed specification.

№01 / COUNT

Yarn count verification

Count measurement and variation checks against the agreed count and tolerance.

〰02 / EVENNESS

Evenness & imperfections

Review of U%, CVm, thin places, thick places and neps using agreed test methods.

↗03 / STRENGTH

Strength & elongation

Assessment of yarn strength, RKM and elongation parameters as applicable.

◌04 / SURFACE

Hairiness & appearance

Visual review of yarn appearance, hairiness, contamination and agreed quality points.

▤05 / SAMPLING

Lot-wise inspection

Sampling across selected cones, cartons or packages to review lot consistency.

✓06 / REPORTING

Inspection reports

Organised observations and test results compared with the provided specification.
