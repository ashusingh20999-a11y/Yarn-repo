Our services

Technical attention to every important parameter.

Inspection scope is customised to yarn type, lot size, buyer requirements, available equipment and agreed sampling plan.

№01 / COUNT

Yarn Count Verification

Count measurement and count variation checks against agreed specifications and tolerances.

〰02 / EVENNESS

Evenness & Imperfections

Review of U%, CVm, thin places, thick places, neps and IPI as per agreed test method.

↗03 / STRENGTH

Strength & Elongation

Assessment of yarn strength, RKM and elongation parameters where included in scope.

◌04 / APPEARANCE

Hairiness & Appearance

Visual review of yarn appearance, hairiness, contamination and other agreed quality points.

▤05 / LOT CHECK

Lot-wise Inspection

Sampling across selected cones, cartons or packages to review consistency within a lot.

✓06 / REPORT

Inspection Reporting

Organised observations and test results compared with the buyer's provided specification.
