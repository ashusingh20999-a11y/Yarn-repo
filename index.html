Our Services

Yarn inspection solutions

Inspection scope can be tailored to the yarn type, buyer requirements and agreed quality parameters.

◎

Yarn Count Verification

Verification of yarn count and count variation against the agreed specification and sampling plan.

⌁

Evenness & Imperfections

Review of U%, CVm, thin places, thick places and neps using suitable testing methods and equipment.

⇄

Strength & Elongation

Assessment of yarn lea strength or single-yarn strength, RKM and elongation as applicable.

◉

Hairiness & Appearance

Inspection of yarn hairiness, visual appearance, contamination and other agreed quality points.

▤

Lot-wise Inspection

Sampling and inspection across selected cones, cartons or packages to evaluate lot consistency.

✓

Inspection Reports

Structured reports with observations, test results and a comparison against the provided specification.

How We Work

A clear inspection process

STEP 01

STEP 02

Plan Sampling

STEP 03
