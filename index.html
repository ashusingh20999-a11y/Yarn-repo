Our services

Technical attention to every important parameter.

Our inspection scope can be customised to yarn type, lot size, buyer requirements, available equipment and the agreed sampling plan.

№01 / COUNT

Yarn Count Verification

Count measurement and count variation checks against agreed specifications and tolerances.

〰02 / EVENNESS

Evenness & Imperfections

Review of U%, CVm, thin places, thick places, neps and IPI as per agreed test method.

↗03 / STRENGTH

Strength & Elongation
